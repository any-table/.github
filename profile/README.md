# any table

A belief system that claims no revelation, owns no buildings, takes no collection, and belongs to no one.

> What we know, we hold with an open hand.
> What we love, we hold with both.
> Tell the truth, do the work, help where you can,
> and do not wait for certainty to begin.

The practice is small enough to carry in your head: twenty minutes of silence a day, and once a week a shared meal at any table with at least one other person. The text behind it is public domain, and anyone may copy it, change it, or fork it.

**Read it:** [the card](https://anytable.org/), one page · [the full text](https://anytable.org/foundational/) · [the sources](https://anytable.org/reading/)

## Repositories

- [**anytable**](https://github.com/any-table/anytable): the canonical text, its revision history, and the governance record. Changes are proposed here by pull request.
- [**directory**](https://github.com/any-table/directory): an ownerless list of tables, by place and time. Add your own; remove it whenever you like.

## Starting a table

You don't need anyone's permission. Read the card, find one other person, pick a table, and keep the weekly gathering. If you want others to find you, add a line to the directory. A table that has met in at least eight of the last twelve weeks counts as active for governance, which is how the project stops being run by its founder.

## Changing the text

Open a pull request against [`FOUNDATIONAL.md`](https://github.com/any-table/anytable/blob/main/FOUNDATIONAL.md). Discussion happens in the open, and the rules for adopting changes are in [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](https://github.com/any-table/anytable/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md). Source corrections and broken links can be fixed right away.

## What not to post here

Nothing said in a table's hard-truth round, no reports of harm, and no one's personal details belong in an issue, pull request, or discussion. This project is not a reporting body. If someone is in danger, contact local emergency services; for abuse or other serious harm, go to the appropriate authority or service where you are. See [On harm within](https://anytable.org/foundational/#on-harm-within).

## Where things stand

This is new. The text is at version 0.x, the project is in its bootstrap period, and the current state of custody and active tables is kept in [`GOVERNANCE.md`](https://github.com/any-table/anytable/blob/main/GOVERNANCE.md). If this ever produces cruelty, coercive certainty, fear, wealth or status for its leaders, obedience to any person, concealment of serious harm, or retaliation against those who speak, it has failed, and the right response is to end it and begin again.
